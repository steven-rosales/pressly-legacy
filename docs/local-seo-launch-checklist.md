# Pressly Local SEO Launch Checklist

This checklist covers the off-site and launch tasks that cannot be fully
enforced in code.

## Launch Hygiene

- Confirm `trypressly.com` resolves to the production AWS/Lightsail address
  before submitting the sitemap.
- Confirm these URLs serve the new Next.js app, not Squarespace:
  - `https://trypressly.com/robots.txt`
  - `https://trypressly.com/sitemap.xml`
  - `https://trypressly.com/new-jersey`
- Submit `https://trypressly.com/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console and Bing
  Webmaster Tools after DNS cutover.
- Check the AWS/Caddy-served Next.js redirect behavior for old Squarespace
  URLs:
  - `/home`
  - `/blog`
  - `/blog/cost-of-wash-and-fold-laundry-service-in-new-jersey`
  - `/schedule-pickup`
  - `/dry-cleaning-landing`
  - `/wash-and-fold-landing`
  - city shortcuts such as `/jersey-city`, `/newark`, `/hoboken`

## Google Business Profile

- Use Pressly as a verified service-area business.
- Keep the booking URL pointed at `https://trypressly.com/schedule`.
- Keep the website URL pointed at `https://trypressly.com/`.
- Set categories and services around laundry service, wash and fold, dry
  cleaning, pickup, and delivery.
- Add service areas that match the website: Bergen, Essex, Hudson, Morris,
  Passaic, and Union counties.
- Add real service photos and refresh photos/posts weekly during launch.
- Keep customer care hours consistent with the website: Mon-Sun, 1pm-10pm.

## Citations And Local Authority

- Use one consistent NAP everywhere:
  - Name: Pressly
  - Phone: (862) 310-6447
  - Website: https://trypressly.com/
  - Email: care@trypressly.com
- Build citations on major directories and local NJ sources.
- Prioritize partnerships and links from apartments, property managers,
  colleges, gyms, salons, hospitality businesses, chambers, and neighborhood
  publications in priority cities.

## Reviews

- Ask every completed order for a Google review.
- Do not script review text or ask for only positive reviews.
- Encourage customers to mention the city and service naturally when relevant.
- Respond to every review with the service and city context when appropriate.

## Weekly Measurement

- Track Google Search Console impressions, clicks, indexed pages, and query
  clusters by template:
  - State hub
  - County hubs
  - City laundry pages
  - City dry cleaning pages
  - Pricing pages
- Compare priority cities first: Jersey City, Hoboken, Newark, Montclair,
  Clifton, Paterson, Hackensack, Fort Lee, Teaneck, Paramus, Bayonne,
  Union City, Bloomfield, West Orange, Morristown, and Elizabeth.
